The open loop

An unfinished story is hard to ignore.

Start a story at its most tense moment and stop before the outcome: "I'll come back to what they changed after that call." Your customer's mind keeps waiting for the ending, and that holds attention through the middle of the demo, where it usually drops. Each scene ends with a one-line tease for the next, so there's always something still to come. At the end you finish the story, and its ending is what they've just seen on screen.

  1. Who is in the roomEach attendee and what they care about
  2. Open the loopA true story told up to the worst moment, then a pause
  3. Outcome firstThe finished result, before any clicking
  4. Three scenesTheir problem, what to show, one "so what" line, a check-in and a tease
  5. Close the loopThe ending, tied to what they just watched
  6. Hand it overOne question, then stop talking
  7. Leave out todayWhat you won't show, and why

One rule

The story has to be true.

Your own experience, or a published customer story you can link to. A made-up story falls apart the moment someone asks "which customer was that?", and it takes your credibility with it. The AI never writes your opening story, and the builder won't clear its checks until you confirm it's true. A story bank keeps your best ones so you can tell them again.
